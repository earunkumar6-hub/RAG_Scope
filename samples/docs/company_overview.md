# Northwind Solar: Company Overview

Northwind Solar is a fictional installer of rooftop photovoltaic systems for small businesses along the North Sea coast. The company was founded in 2014 in Bergen, Norway, by Maya Lindqvist, an electrical engineer who previously designed offshore wind substations. It opened a second office in Rotterdam in 2019 to serve customers in the Netherlands and Belgium.

## Products

Northwind sells three product lines:

- **NW-400 panel**: a 400-watt monocrystalline module with an anodised aluminium frame and salt-mist certification. It is the default panel on every coastal job.
- **Tidewater inverter**: a string inverter made by Kestrel Power Systems, used on roofs without shading.
- **Tidewater Micro**: a microinverter from the same supplier, fitted one per panel on roofs that are partly shaded.

Battery storage uses the **Fjord LFP-10**, a 10 kWh lithium iron phosphate battery supplied by Aster Cells.

## Organisation

Maya Lindqvist is the chief executive. Jonas Verhoeven leads the Rotterdam office and the installation crews in the Netherlands. Priya Raman is head of customer service and owns the warranty process and the customer portal. Field technicians are trained at the Bergen workshop before they work on customer roofs.

## Partners

Kestrel Power Systems supplies all inverters and provides a ten-year inverter warranty that Northwind passes on to customers. Aster Cells supplies batteries. Harbour Leasing finances the solar lease programme, under which Northwind keeps ownership of the equipment.

## Sustainability

Northwind recycles decommissioned panels through a take-back scheme run with the Bergen municipal recycling centre. In 2025 the company installed 1,240 systems with a combined capacity of 18.6 megawatts.
